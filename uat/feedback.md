# UAT feedback: product list

Round 2, Acme Supply product list. Tested on the QA build by three testers on phones and laptops. Fictional, written for this demo.

| # | Tester | Device | What they saw | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | T1 | Phone, 4G | The page jumps when the list loads. I tapped "Sign up" at the bottom and hit a product card instead, because everything moved. | High |
| 2 | T2 | Laptop | A few grey blocks show first, then the real cards come in much taller, and the footer drops off the screen. | Medium |
| 3 | T1 | Phone, 4G | While I scroll, cards keep growing as their pictures arrive. I lose my place. | High |
| 4 | T3 | Phone, train wifi | The prices and titles flick to a different font a second after the page shows, and the text moves. | Low |
| 5 | T2 | Laptop | The green "Free shipping" bar appears late at the top and shoves the whole page down. | Medium |
| 6 | T3 | Phone, train wifi | Overall it feels broken on a slow connection. It looked fine on the office wifi. | High |

Result: not accepted. Retest after fixes.
